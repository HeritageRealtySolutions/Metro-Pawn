Our Story

About Collateral

The Brand

"We built Collateral because trust was missing from this industry. Every piece that crosses our counter gets treated like it matters — because to someone, it does."

Collateral is a premium pawn shop rooted in Waller, TX. We serve buyers, sellers, and borrowers across the greater Houston area who want honest appraisals and fair terms — no theatrics, no pressure.

Our specialists are trained in jewelry, watches, electronics, firearms, and collectibles. When you bring something in, you get a real number from a real person who knows what they're talking about.

We've been operating in Waller long enough to know our customers by name. That's how we like it.

What We Stand For

  • 01

    Transparency

    Every appraisal is explained. Every number has a reason. You leave knowing exactly what we paid and why.

  • 02

  • 03

    Respect

    People bring us things that matter to them. We treat every transaction with the weight it deserves.

  • 04

    Fairness

    We don't win by taking advantage. We build long-term relationships. Repeat business is our proof of concept.
